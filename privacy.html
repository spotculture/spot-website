Privacy policy

Who we are

Spot is an independent app made in France. Data controller and publisher: Selma Attie, France. Contact: app.support.spot@gmail.com. Website host: GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

Without an account

Spot keeps everything on your phone: your city, saved shows, followed venues, your taste. Your location is used on the phone only, to show what's near you, and is never sent to us.

With an account

If you sign in with Apple, we store in Apple's iCloud: your name, @username, optional photo, bio and home city; who you follow and who follows you; and, if you allow it, the shows you save.

Why

To run the account and following features you asked for. Nothing is used for advertising, sold, or shared with third parties. Spot has no ads and no tracking.

How long

Until you delete your account. “Delete my account” in the app erases everything right away.

Your rights (GDPR)

You can see and export your data, correct it, delete it, and object or complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Age

You must be 15 or older to create an account.

Where

Account data is stored by Apple (iCloud) under Apple's data processing terms. Listings contain no personal data. To show listings and photos, your phone connects to GitHub Pages (USA) and to venues' and image sites, which can see your IP address as with any website. Apple and GitHub may process data outside the EU under their own safeguards.
