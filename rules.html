Community rules

Be kind

Spot is for sharing what you loved (or didn't) about a show. Reviews, tips, names, bios and photos must not insult, harass, threaten or mock anyone, and must not attack people for who they are (origin, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, age).

Keep it clean and legal

No sexual or violent content, no hateful symbols, no spam or advertising, no personal details about other people, and nothing you don't have the right to post.

Keep it about culture

Write about the show, the venue and the visit. Criticism of a show is welcome; attacks on people are not.

What happens otherwise

Spot filters insults automatically. Anyone can report a review or a profile, or block its author. Reports are checked within 24 hours: content that breaks these rules is removed, and accounts that break them again are closed. Spot has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.

Contact

To report something urgently or appeal a decision: app.support.spot@gmail.com.
